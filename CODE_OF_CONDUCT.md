# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Faultline to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully; critique code, not
  people.
- Help newcomers. Everyone started somewhere.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Publishing others' private information without their permission.
- Trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests,
discussions and reviews, and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainer through their GitHub profile,
[JulianChaoul16](https://github.com/JulianChaoul16). Reports will be
reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, code or issues that break this
code, and may warn, temporarily ban, or permanently ban anyone whose
behaviour is harmful to the community.
